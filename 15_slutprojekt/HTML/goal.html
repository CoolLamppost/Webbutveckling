In the game you play as a robot who descends into hell to get blood, because in this universe robots are driven by blood for some reason, therefore, blood (killing enemies) is one of the only ways to regain health. The "main" gameplay loop often consists of beating a level to try to get a score in the following categories: kills (how many enemies you killed), time (how little time it took you) and "style"(how cool you are basically). Getting a high enough score in all of the categories will yield you a "P" rank
