you play as a robot who descends into hell to get blood because in this universe robots are driven by blood for some reason

the "main" gameplay loop consists of beating the different levels in the game and attempting to get a score in the categories: kills (how many enemies you killed), time (how long/short time it took you) and "style"(how cool you look)

getting a high enough score in all of the categories will yield you a "perfect" rating
